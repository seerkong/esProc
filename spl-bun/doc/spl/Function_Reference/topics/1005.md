## 提取内容
workday()
说明：
Find
a date which is the n th
workday after the specified date.
语法：
workday ( t , k,h )
注意：
The
function finds a date which is the k th
workday after the date t . The h is a sequence of holidays and/or
workdays, whose members are weekend and/or workdays. The specified weekend will
become workdays and vice versa.
参数：
|
t
|
A date value .
|
k
|
An integer .
|
h
|
A sequence composed of values of date data
type .
选项：
|
@b
|
Use
binary search algorithm when sequence h is ordered.
返回值：
日期
示例：
|
workday(date("2011-11-07"),25,[date("2011-12-03"),date("2011-12-31")])
|
2011-12-09
|
workday(date("2011-11-07"),25,[date("2011-11-30"),date("2011-12-31")])
|
2011-12-13
