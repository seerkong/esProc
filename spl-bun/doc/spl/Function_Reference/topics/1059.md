## 提取内容
Union of sequences
说明：
The
operation generates a new sequence by merging two sequences.
语法：
A & B
A & x
x & A
注意：
Generate
a new sequence by merging the members (or single values) from two sequences A and B in proper order. The
common members will not appear repeatedly in the new sequence.
参数：
|
A sequence with
the length of n .
|
A sequence
with the length of m or a single value; When it is a single value, it
is regarded as [ B ] .
返回值：
new sequence after merging the two sequences A and B
示例：
|
=[1,2,3,4]&[3,2]
|
[1,2,3,4] . If members of the two sequences are not arranged in the same
order, then follow the order of the first sequence.
|
=[1,2,3,3]&[1,3]
|
[1,2,3,3] . Duplicate members will not appear repeatedly, but the same
members in the former sequence will be retained.
|
=[1,2,3,3]&4
|
[1,2,3,3,4]
|
=4&[1,2,3,3]
|
[4,1,2,3,3]
