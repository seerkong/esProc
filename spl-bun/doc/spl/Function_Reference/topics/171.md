comconj()
Descpription:
Calculate
a complex number’s complex conjugate.
语法：
A .comconj()
注意：
The
MathCli external library function (See
External Library Guide ) returns the complex conjugate of each member
of the specified sequence of complex numbers. For two complex numbers, one is
the other’s complex conjugate if they have same real parts and their imaginary
parts are opposite numbers. If a complex number’s imaginary part is 0, its
complex conjugate’s imaginary part is also 0.
参数：
|
sequence of complex numbers.
Return
value:
Sequence
