compair()
说明：
Sort
complex numbers in the form of complex conjugate pairs.
语法：

注意：
The MathCli external library function (See External Library Guide ) sorts the specified sequence of complex numbers while putting together
complex numbers that are complex conjugates to each other.
The function sorts pairs of complex
conjugates by their real parts in ascending order – for each pair, the element
having a negative imaginary part goes ahead, and returns pure 实数
values after all pairs of complex conjugates. If two pairs of complex
conjugates have same real part, sort them by absolute numbers in the imaginary
parts in descending order and according to the rule that the negative imaginary
number goes ahead. As in the first case, put 实数s in ascending order
after all pairs of complex conjugates.
注意：
Complex numbers should be input in the form of complex conjugate pairs.

sequence of complex numbers.
