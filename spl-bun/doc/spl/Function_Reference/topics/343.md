## 提取内容
Fintrate()
说明：
The
function equals the Excel INTRATE function.
语法：
Fintrate ( settlement,maturity,investment,redemption )
注意：
The
FinancialCli external library
function (See External Library Guide ) returns
the interest rate for a security that pays interest at maturity, with the day
count basis method as US (NASD) 30/360 .
参数：
|
settlement
|
The security's settlement date.
|
maturity
|
The security's maturity date.
|
investment
|
The initial amount invested into the
security.
|
redemption
|
The security's redemption value.
Option ：
|
@1
|
Specify
the day count basis method Actual/Actual . It
corresponds to the Excel basis
parameter .
|
@0
|
Specify
the day count basis method Actual/360 . It
corresponds to the Excel basis
parameter .
|
@5
|
Specify
the day count basis method Actual/365 . It
corresponds to the Excel basis
parameter .
|
@e
|
Specify
the day count basis method European
30/360 . It corresponds to the Excel basis parameter .
示例：
|
Fintrate@0(date("2001-2-15"),date("2001-5-15"),1000000,1014420)
|
0.058328089887640454
