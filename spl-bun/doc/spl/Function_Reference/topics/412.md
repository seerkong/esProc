说明：
Perform row-to-column/column-to-row
transposition on member sequences of a sequence.
语法：

注意：
The function performs
row-to-column/column-to-row transposition on member sequences of sequence A ; default is row-to-column transposition.
Members of sequence A are also sequences. Both g and v are sequences
consisting of sequence numbers of certain members of member sequences of A . Use syntax ~( i ) or ~ i to reference a member of the
member sequence of A .

v to corresponding sequences in each
group and return a sequence of sequences.
