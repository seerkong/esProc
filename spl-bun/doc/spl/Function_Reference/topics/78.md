age()

Compute
the number of whole years between two time points.
语法：
age( dateExp1 : formatExp , dateExp2 : formatExp )

respectively defined by parameter dateExp1 and parameter dateExp2 .
参数：
|
dateExp1/dateExp2
|

is by default the result of now().
|

"yyyyMMdd","yyyy-MM-dd" .
选项：
|
@y

Accurate
to the year.
|
@m
|
Accurate
to the month; accurate to the day by default.
返回值：
Integer

|
=age(date("1980-09-01"))
