## 提取内容
ranks()
说明：
Get
the rankings of members of a sequence .
语法：
|
A .ranks( x )
|
注意：
The function
computes expression x over each
member of sequence A to return the rankings
of members of sequence A.(x) , which
by default is sorted in ascending order.
参数：
|
A sequence .
|
x
|
An
expression; cannot be omitted when A is a table sequence or a record sequence
选项：
|
@z
|
Return a ranking
in descending order .
|
@i
|
Deduplicate
sequence A. ( x ) before getting the rankings .
|
@s
|
Perform
average operation on rankings of duplicate members and return the final
result, which could contain non-integers .
|
@o
|
Use
this option to increase efficiency when sequence A is order.
返回值：
Integer sequence
示例：
When
A is a sequence:
|
=[2,1,3,4,8,5,2,0]
|
=A1.ranks()
|
[3,2,5,6,8,7,3,1]; rankings
in ascending order .
|
=A1.ranks@z()
|
[5,7,4,3,1,2,5,8]; rankings
in descending order .
|
=A1.ranks@i()
|
[3,2,4,5,7,6,3,1]; remove duplicate members and
then get rankings .
|
=A1.ranks@s()
|
[3.5,2.0,5.0,6.0,8.0,7.0,3.5,1.0]
compute average of rankings of duplicate members and then get rankings. That
is, there are two members with value 2 and
their rankings are 3 and 4 respectively and the result of average operation
is (3+4)/2=3.5; so ranking of 2 in the sequence is 3.5.
