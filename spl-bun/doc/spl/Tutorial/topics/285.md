### 12.3.2 日期轴

日期轴与数值轴类似。但其逻辑坐标是日期/时间数据，而不是数值。日期轴通常用于绘制趋势图、运行图等图表。

以下是股票趋势图的绘图算法：

|
=canvas()
|
=demo.query("select
* from STOCKRECORDS where STOCKID='002242'")
|
=A1.plot("BackGround")
|
=A1.plot("DateAxis","name":"x","format":"dd/M","displayStep":20)
|
=A1.plot("NumericAxis","name":"y","location":2,"autoCalcValueRange":
false,"maxValue":50.0)
|
=A1.plot("Line","markerWeight":2,"axis1":"x","data1":A2.(DATE),"axis2":
"y","data2":A2.(CLOSING))
|
=A1.draw@p(450,250)
