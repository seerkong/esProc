### 12.3.4 极坐标系中的极轴和径向轴

极坐标系中的极轴或径向轴也可以是数值轴、枚举轴或日期轴。但逻辑轴的属性与笛卡尔坐标系中逻辑轴的属性不同。

以下是跳马成绩饼图的绘图算法：

|
=canvas()
|
=demo.query("select
* from GYMSCORE where EVENT = 'Vault'")

|
=A1.plot("EnumAxis","name":"x","location":3,"polarX":0.5,"allowLabels":false)
|
=A1.plot("NumericAxis","name":"y","location":4,"allowLabels":false)
|
=A1.plot("Sector","text":A2.(NAME),"axis1":"x","data1":A2.(NAME),
"axis2":"y","data2":A2.(SCORE))
|
=A1.draw@p(450,350)
