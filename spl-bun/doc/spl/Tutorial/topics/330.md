### 12.11.4 双轴图形

您可以使用双轴图形来绘制双轴柱线图或双轴折线图。以下是双轴柱线图的绘图算法：

|
=canvas()
|
=demo.query("select
* from STATES where STATEID<6")
|
=A2.new(ABBR,"Population":Type,POPULATION/1000000:Value)
|
=A2.new(ABBR,"Area":Type,AREA/1000:Value)
|
=A3|A4
|
=A1.plot("Graph2Axis","categories":A5.(ABBR+","+Type),"values":
A5.(Value)"yTitle":"Population(M);Area(K sq.
mi)","yTitleAngle":90,"axisRightColor":-3355444)
|
=A1.draw@p(500,250)
